rclone · Personal Google Drive client

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026.

This policy covers the personal Google OAuth client named rclone, configured by prok20 to use rclone with their own Google Drive account.

Data accessed and purpose

Within the permissions granted by the owner, rclone accesses Google Drive file contents and metadata, such as file names, identifiers, sizes, and modification times. This access is used to carry out the owner's file transfer, synchronization, and backup commands. Write or delete operations are possible only when the granted permissions allow them.

Where data is processed and stored

Rclone runs on devices or servers selected and controlled by the owner and communicates with Google's APIs. Downloaded files, backups, caches, and any configured logs remain on the devices or storage destinations chosen by the owner. Files are retained until the owner deletes them or changes the relevant retention settings.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in rclone's configuration on the machine running rclone, unless the owner configures another supported storage method. Tokens are used to maintain the authorized connection and remain there until the owner removes them. They must be protected as credentials.

Sharing and other uses

The client does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Files are transferred only as directed by the owner, including to any other storage provider they explicitly configure as a destination. The client has no separate operator-run service collecting Drive data.

Revoking access and deleting local data

The owner can revoke the client's access at any time through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not delete files already downloaded or copied. To remove retained data, the owner must also delete the relevant rclone configuration entries, local copies, caches, logs, and backups on their devices or configured destinations.

This website

These informational pages contain no sign-in form, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies and do not receive Google OAuth tokens or Drive data. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and changes

For questions about this personal client, see the owner's GitHub profile. Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
